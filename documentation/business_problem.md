# Business Problem

Retail leaders need a trusted view of sales performance, orders, customers, delivery status, refunds, returns, inventory position, and promotion activity. The available source extracts span these business areas but require quality assessment and a governed semantic model before dashboard conclusions can be trusted.

The report answers four questions:

1. How are authoritative order-level sales and order volume changing over time?
2. Which cities, branches, payment methods, delivery statuses, and customers drive sales performance?
3. What is the scale and primary cause of return and refund activity?
4. What can product inventory and promotion configuration reveal without overstating incomplete product-line order coverage?

`orders_data.csv` is the authoritative sales source. Product order-line data is incomplete and is excluded from enterprise sales and profit reporting.
