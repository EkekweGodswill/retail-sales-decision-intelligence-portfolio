# Business Insights

## Sales trend

Authoritative sales total $250.47M across 5,000 orders. Sales were $45.47M in 2024, $123.16M in 2025, and $81.84M in 2026. The 2026 figure should not be treated as a full-year comparison without confirming the reporting-period cutoff.

## Channel and branch performance

Card payments generated $86.51M, followed by cash at $84.16M and wallet at $79.80M. Mansoura was the highest-sales branch at $61.18M, while Alexandria was lowest at $35.12M.

## Returns and refunds

There were 425 returned orders, 1,000 return transactions, and $2.55M in refunds. Wrong item was the largest refund reason at $0.70M, followed by damaged product at $0.64M. These reasons should be investigated for fulfilment, product-handling, and listing-quality causes.

## Promotion mix

Seasonal promotions accounted for $92.56M in order-level sales, the largest promotion type in the current extract. Promotion sales show order revenue associated with promotion IDs; they do not prove incremental lift or discount profitability.

## Inventory and product scope

Product inventory can support stock-position analysis. Product profit, gross margin, and turnover cannot support enterprise conclusions because the order-item extract covers only 9.4% of orders and does not reconcile to authoritative order totals.
